1. Controller and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the Maestro app for Android (package com.jellas.maestroremote) handles information about its users. The app is developed and published by JellasStudio ("JellasStudio", "we"), which acts as the data controller under Brazilian Law No. 13,709/2018 (General Data Protection Law — LGPD) and, where applicable, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

By using Maestro, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. It applies only to the app; third-party services mentioned here have their own policies.

2. Definitions

  • Personal data: information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Data subject: the person the personal data refers to — you.
  • Processing: any operation performed on data, such as collection, use, storage, sharing and deletion.
  • Compatible device: any equipment Maestro can control, such as TVs, streaming devices, receivers, speakers, air conditioners and other connected devices.
  • Local network: the Wi-Fi network your phone and your devices are connected to.
  • Free plan / Maestro Pro: ways of using the app without and with a subscription or lifetime purchase.

Device discovery uses standard local network protocols, such as mDNS and SSDP, and is limited to the network your phone is connected to. App icons shown belong to their respective owners and are used for identification only.

Devices controlled through the manufacturer's cloud

Some devices only accept commands through the manufacturer's own cloud service. If you choose to connect your account for that service to Maestro, authorization takes place on the manufacturer's official page, and Maestro never has access to your password. The app keeps on your phone only the access code granted and sends commands directly to the manufacturer's servers, whose data processing follows the manufacturer's privacy policy. You can disconnect the account at any time in the app or on the manufacturer's website.

4. Voice commands

Maestro does not record, store or transmit audio. When you tap the microphone button, the app invokes the Android system's speech recognition service, usually provided by Google. That service captures the audio and returns to Maestro only the recognized text (for example, "open YouTube"), which is used immediately to run the command and then discarded.

Audio processing by the voice service provider is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The microphone is never activated without an action on your part.

5. Advertising

The free plan shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the device list and in settings and, occasionally, an ad when you return to the app after a few hours away. The remote control screen never shows ads. For Maestro Pro subscribers, the ad service is not started.

To show ads, cap their frequency and measure them, AdMob may collect the Android advertising ID, IP address, technical information about the device and system, and interactions with ads. This data is processed by Google as an independent controller, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In jurisdictions where the law requires prior consent (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your permission before loading ads, through Google's official consent platform, and lets you review it at any time under Settings > About > Ad privacy options. In any country, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Maestro Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed exclusively by Google Play. JellasStudio does not receive or store your name, email, address, card details or any other payment information.

The app receives from Google Play only confirmation that an active purchase exists, together with a transaction code, in order to unlock Pro features. Cancellations, refunds and subscription management are handled in the Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions, according to the Google Play Terms of Service.

8. Sharing and international transfers

JellasStudio does not sell, rent or share personal data. The only third parties that may receive data are the Google services described in sections 4 to 6, your own devices on the local network and, only if you connect your account, the respective manufacturer's cloud service (section 3).

Google may process data on servers located outside Brazil. These transfers are carried out by Google itself, based on the safeguards provided for in art. 33 of the LGPD, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Storage, retention and security

The data described in section 3 remains on your device while the app is installed. You can delete it at any time by removing a device from the list, clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling Maestro. Data processed by Google follows the retention periods set out in its policy.

Pairing keys are kept in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps. Communication with devices uses encryption (TLS) whenever the manufacturer supports it, as on Samsung, LG and Android TV televisions, and communication with cloud services is always encrypted. No system is completely immune to failure; if a relevant security incident occurs, data subjects and the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) will be notified as required by art. 48 of the LGPD.

11. Your rights

Under art. 18 of the LGPD, you may, at any time and free of charge, request:

  • confirmation that processing takes place and access to the data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully;
  • data portability;
  • information about the entities with which data is shared;
  • information about the possibility of not giving consent and its consequences;
  • withdrawal of consent and deletion of data processed on that basis.

Because Maestro's data stays on your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in Android settings. For data processed by Google, use the tools in your Google Account. Users subject to the GDPR also have the rights to object to and restrict processing. You may also file a complaint with the ANPD or with the data protection authority of your country.

Requests can be sent to the email address in section 14 and will be answered within 15 days.

12. Children

Maestro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child's data has been processed without a guardian's consent, please contact us so it can be deleted.
